# Privacy policy

Auto Dark Mode for Gmail does not collect, store or share any data.

- It runs only on Gmail (`mail.google.com`) and on the account menu, apps menu and contact cards Gmail shows from `ogs.google.com` and `contacts.google.com`.
- It only changes how these pages look, following your system's light or dark mode. To decide what to darken, it looks at the colours and layout of the page inside your browser. In dark mode this includes checking whether text on the page, email text included, is blank and what colour it is. None of that text is stored or sent anywhere.
- It makes no network requests, uses no analytics and stores nothing, neither in your browser nor anywhere else.
- Its only permission is access to the three sites above, which your browser shows when you install it. It asks for no other permissions.

Questions: https://github.com/daaain/Gmail-System-Theme-mode/issues
